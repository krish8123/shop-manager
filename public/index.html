➕ Add / stock a product

Enter the wholesale (cost) price you paid and how many pieces you bought. The retail price is optional — it's just a suggested default. You set the actual selling price for each customer at the time of sale, so you can sell the same item at ₹150 to one customer and ₹180 to another.
💡 Tip: To restock an existing product, just add it again with the same name — quantities add up automatically.

⚙️ Data & backup

All your data is saved privately in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use backup to keep a copy or move to another device.
⚠️ Clearing browser data / history may erase your records — download a backup regularly to stay safe.

❓ How to use

1. Inventory tab → Add each product with the wholesale price you paid and quantity.
2. Sales tab → When a customer buys, pick the product, enter the retail price they paid. Profit is calculated and stock reduces automatically.
3. Expenses tab → Log shop costs like rent and electricity.
4. Dashboard → See your total revenue, profit, stock value and net profit any time.
